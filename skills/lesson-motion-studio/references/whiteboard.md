# Whiteboard visual and motion specification

Use a white canvas and black marker outlines with one or two accent colors
Use light irregular hatching only where it helps identify a material or emphasis
Keep Cairo for Arabic and English labels rather than copying handwritten fonts from reference thumbnails
Choose one focal drawing per beat and build it in meaningful parts

Create vector paths or separated raster stroke layers for the scene
Reveal each path from start to end at approximately constant arc length with eased entry and exit
Place the pen tip at the current revealed endpoint with an offset calibrated to the pen asset
Hide or lift the pen while repositioning between disconnected paths
Keep the hand below captions in visual priority and outside the active reading area
Do not draw an entire detailed illustration while narrating an unrelated sentence
Shape complete Arabic words with Cairo and RTL before tracing their ink paths
Reveal shaped glyph strokes gradually in reading direction with pen contact and retain connected final forms and diacritics
Hold completed words for reading and keep static interface labels separate from animated teaching text
Hold the finished drawing to allow reading before adding another element

For a grammar example draw the situation then add the sentence in meaningful units
For a missing predicate draw an empty space then insert the predicate and underline its relation
For mathematics draw exact equal parts and map quantities to symbols
For science draw accurate relationships with deterministic paths and mark simplified representations

An opacity fade or rectangular wipe over a static picture is not enough to claim stroke drawing
If a generated illustration cannot be decomposed reliably use it as a finished supporting drawing and render the main teaching paths natively
For a hand cutout use imagegen or a licensed asset and inspect fingers pen grip alpha and contact point
Review frames during drawing not only completed frames
Check pen contact continuity text readability and silence before quiz answers

Use circles to isolate the target unit arrows to show a meaningful relation and translucent highlighting behind readable ink
Avoid simultaneous competing markers and paths crossing other captions
Keep all answer hints absent during the thinking interval
